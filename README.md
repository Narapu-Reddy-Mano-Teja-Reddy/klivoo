# ✨ Why KLIVOO?

KLIVOO is an all-in-one CRM platform built for agencies, startups, freelancers, and enterprises to manage their complete business workflow from one dashboard.

💙 Manage Clients
📁 Projects & Retainers
📅 Meetings & Calendar
💰 Billing & Payments
✅ Tasks & Teams
📂 Documents
📈 Analytics
🤖 AI Automation
