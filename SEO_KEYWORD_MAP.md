## Secondary / long-tail keyword placement (used naturally in body copy)

- **Location modifiers** (India, Indian freelancers, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai) — used only where genuinely relevant, primarily on `/for/indian-freelancers`, the India-specific tools/glossary, and blog posts. No thin city-spam pages were created (deliberate decision).
- **Long-tail question queries** (how to invoice clients in India, how to send a proposal, tools for solo agency owners, free tools for freelancers India, how to organise freelance business, etc.) are answered within the most relevant tool, glossary, blog, or audience page body + FAQ, and captured by FAQPage schema.
